Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

AIConnectPro (“we”, “us”) is operated by AIConnectpro Support. This notice explains what information the service handles, how it is used, and how you can request its deletion.

Information we handle

  • Account details: email address, sign-in identifiers, and account type or role.
  • Profile and resume details: information you choose to enter, such as your name, profile photo, date of birth, contact details, location, skills, education, work history, and professional summary.
  • Service activity: job applications, posts, reels, reports you submit, employer access requests, and information you submit through forms.
  • Technical information: basic request and device information processed by our hosting and authentication providers to operate and protect the service.

How we use information

We use this information to provide sign-in, profiles, resume tools, job listings and applications, employer features, user support, security, and service improvements. If an AI feature is enabled and you choose to use it, the text you submit for that feature may be processed by our AI provider to return a response.

Sharing and visibility

We do not sell personal information. We use service providers to host the site and API, store data, authenticate accounts, and provide AI features when enabled. These providers process information to deliver their services. Members can report Reels or profiles for administrator review; submitting a report does not automatically hide or remove content.

Posts and reels may be visible to other users according to the visibility options shown when you create them. Information in a job application, including the resume you select, is shared with the employer receiving that application. Do not include information in a post or application that you do not want its intended audience to see.

Your profile is not a public directory. Discoverability is off by default; when you turn it on, signed-in members can find your name and any company name shown on your profile. Date of birth, location, bio, email, phone number, and profile photo are private by default. In Settings, you can choose who can see each detail: only you, your accepted connections, or everyone signed in to AIConnectPro. “Everyone” does not include logged-out visitors. Saved resumes are private to your account unless you choose one to share with an employer through a job application.

Third-party services

AIConnectPro currently relies on providers for website and API hosting, database and authentication services, and Google sign-in when you choose that option. AI features may also use an AI provider when enabled. Those providers’ own privacy notices govern their services.

Retention and deletion

We keep account and submitted information while your account is active and as needed to provide the service. In Settings, you can download an export of the account information and uploaded files associated with your account, or start account deletion. Deletion requires recent sign-in and typed confirmation. Accounts that own an organization under review or already verified must transfer or close that organization before deletion can continue. Admins must transfer administrator access before deletion. You can contact sricharankolakani@gmail.com with the subject “AIConnectPro data deletion” if you cannot use the self-service option.

Deleting an account removes its account data, uploaded files, posts, resumes, and applications. It also removes direct conversations and their messages for both participants. Provider-managed logs or backups may retain copies according to each provider’s retention rules, which vary by provider and service plan. We do not promise a single backup deletion period across providers.

Some information may need to be retained where required for security, dispute resolution, or legal obligations. Where retention is necessary, we will restrict it to that purpose and delete it when no longer needed.

Your choices

You can choose what profile and resume information to enter, turn profile discovery on or off, set visibility for each profile detail, choose who can see a post or reel using the options shown when you publish, and choose which resume to attach when applying. You can also request access, correction, or deletion by contacting us. Sign-in through Google is optional; you can use another available sign-in method.

Security and children

We use access controls and provider security features designed to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. AIConnectPro is not designed for children under 18; please do not create an account if you are under 18.

Changes and contact

We may update this notice as the service changes. We will post the current version on this page with an updated effective date. For privacy questions or requests, contact AIConnectpro Support at sricharankolakani@gmail.com.
